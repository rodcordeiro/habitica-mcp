# Domain

Purpose: Habitica as **daily execution** (habits, dailies, todos, rewards) exposed to agents through MCP — not a project backlog manager.

Product language: follow `CONTEXT.md` (`Item de execução`, `Afazer`, `Diária`, `Hábito`, `Recompensa`, `Planejamento do dia`).

Boundaries:

- Planning / issues / sprints stay outside Habitica (Obsidian, GitHub, boards, etc.).
- Day plan materializes an already-decided daily list; do not import full project backlogs.
- Do not invent business rules; mark uncertain behavior as hypothesis or omit.
